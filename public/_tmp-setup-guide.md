One guide, every skill, any AI. Every RealToolbox skill is a complete set of operating instructions that turns a general AI into a real estate specialist that follows one job, conservatively, the way an operator would.

There are two ways to run your toolbox: connect it (the best way) or copy it (works everywhere). Both are included.

## Connect your toolbox via MCP

RealToolbox isn't a folder of files — it's a live toolbox your AI can plug into. We run a built-in integration (an MCP connection) that lets AI apps sign in as you and pull any skill you own, on demand, always the current version.

What that means in practice:

- No copying, no pasting, no stale versions. Ask your AI to "load my Deal Analyzer from RealToolbox" and it pulls the real thing.
- When we improve a skill you own, you have the new version instantly. Nothing to re-download.
- Your paid skills stay gated to your account — the connection is authenticated, not a public link.

To connect (Claude — the flagship experience):

1. In Claude: Settings → Connectors → Add custom connector.
2. Paste your RealToolbox connection URL (shown below and on your account page).
3. Sign in with your RealToolbox account when prompted. Done — Claude can now search and load every skill you own.

Also connects to: ChatGPT, Cursor, Windsurf, and any AI app that supports MCP connectors. Setup lives in each app's connector/integration settings using the same RealToolbox URL and sign-in. Support and setup steps vary by app and plan — if your app doesn't take connectors yet, the copy method below works everywhere, always.

## Copy and paste — works everywhere

Works on every AI, every plan, every device — nothing to configure.

1. Open the skill on RealToolbox and hit Copy skill.
2. Start a new chat in ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini.
3. Paste the entire skill as your first message and send it.
4. The skill introduces itself and asks for what it needs. Answer it, and you're working.

Make it reusable:

- **ChatGPT (Plus):** paste the skill into a Project's instructions — every chat in that Project starts loaded.
- **Claude (Pro):** paste it into a Project's instructions or knowledge. Same effect.
- **Gemini:** create a Gem with the skill as its instructions.

Free tiers work fine for all three; paid tiers get stronger models and longer sessions — worth it if you run deals daily.

## Rules of the road

**One skill per chat.** Skills are specialists on purpose. Don't stack two in one conversation — the boundaries between them are part of the safety design. When a skill routes you onward ("take this to the Deal Analyzer"), open a fresh chat and load that one.

**One deal per chat.** Start clean for each property. Carryover numbers from a previous deal are how mistakes happen.

**Paste the whole thing.** The guardrails — anti-fabrication rules, conservative defaults, verdict logic — live throughout the file. A partial paste is a broken tool. Never edit the skill body.

**Let it ask.** Load a skill with no deal attached and it greets you and asks for exactly what it needs. That's by design. Answer in one message and it goes to work.

**It will not look things up — good.** RealToolbox skills are built to never invent comps, tax bills, rents, or records. When a skill flags a number as unverified, that's the skill protecting you. Verify flagged inputs before money moves.

**Updates are included.** Connected toolboxes update instantly; copy-paste users simply copy the latest version next time. Nothing to re-buy.

## If something seems off

- The AI ignores the skill's format → you're in a long, cluttered chat. Start fresh and re-load.
- The AI invents property data → it isn't running the full skill. Re-paste the complete file, or reconnect and pull it fresh.
- The skill refuses to give a verdict → your inputs aren't good enough yet. That's the product working. Give it what it asked for.

## Why the skills behave this way

RealToolbox skills are built to never invent comps, tax bills, rents, or records, to flag any number that hasn't been verified, and to refuse a verdict when the inputs aren't good enough to support one. Every skill is torture-tested across ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Meta until they converge on the same answer before it ships. Conservative by design — a tool that tells you what you want to hear is worse than no tool at all.
